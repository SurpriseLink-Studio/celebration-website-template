Privacy Policy

This policy describes how SurpriseLink Studio handles information shared with us when you enquire about or order a personalised celebration website.

Please note: This is an initial privacy notice for direct enquiries. The website currently does not contain an online order form, account registration or payment form. We will update this notice if we add those features or use additional service providers.

1. Information we may receive

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number, email address or social-media handle.
  • Order details such as occasion, package, delivery preferences and instructions.
  • Content you choose to provide for the website, such as names, event details, messages, photographs and other media.
  • Messages and files you send when communicating with us.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, create and revise your celebration website, deliver the agreed service, provide customer support and maintain basic business records.

3. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell customer personal information. Information may be handled by service providers needed to communicate, store project files, host or publish a website, or process a payment you choose to make. We aim to share only what is reasonably needed for the service. A completed celebration website may itself display information or photographs you asked us to publish; please check and approve its contents before it is shared.

4. Photos and permissions

You must have the right and any necessary permission to provide photographs, videos, music or personal details for inclusion in a website. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information that is not needed for the project. Tell us promptly if content should be corrected or taken down.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep enquiry and project information only for as long as reasonably needed to discuss, deliver and support the service, maintain business records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal requirements. Retention periods may differ by record type. You may contact us to request deletion of project materials that are no longer required; some records may need to be retained where the law requires it or where they are needed to establish or defend a claim.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit access to customer materials and use appropriate account and access controls. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through ordinary messages.

7. Your requests

You may contact us to ask what information you have shared, request correction of inaccurate details, ask for deletion where appropriate, or request removal of content from a website we manage. We may need to verify that the request comes from the relevant customer before acting.

8. Children

Our services are ordered by adults. If a celebration website includes a child's name, image or other personal information, the person placing the order must ensure they have the authority and any required parent or guardian permission to share it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact SurpriseLink Studio through WhatsApp at +91 85035 065015 or @surpriselink.studio. Business location: New Delhi, India.
